# Hero photo credits

Photos for the home hero slides, from Wikimedia Commons. All taken in Cambodia, except the mosquito close-up, which shows no place. Each file was downloaded at up to 3840px and resized to 2560px wide WebP (modified from the original). Small background figures in `phnom-penh-rubbish.webp` were blurred for privacy. Mapped to slides in `src/features/home/heroImages.ts`.
